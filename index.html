How to Practice — Try-Speed

Getting Started

Click or tap anywhere on the typing arena to begin. The timer starts automatically when you type the very first character—there is no countdown anxiety.

Durations & Unlimited Mode (inf)

Choose 30s, 60s, 120s, or click Custom to enter any exact duration in seconds (e.g. 15, 45, 90) or type inf for endless zen practice!

Difficulty Levels

  • Easy: High-frequency lowercase words for warming up and muscle memory.
  • Medium: Randomized English words with capitalization, commas, and periods.
  • Hard: Advanced vocabulary, numbers, dates, parentheses, and mixed punctuation.
